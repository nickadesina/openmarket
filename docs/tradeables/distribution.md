# Distribution: featuring someone's work

The X account (@zetsurui) can put a piece in front of a markets-native
audience. This is a real offer to a junior investor, a newsletter or a
podcast host, and a small one; say so. The rules below follow the
account's playbook: originals outperform quotes, a quote only rides a
post that has already escaped, the text must be specific to the piece,
no image stacked on a quote.

## Three post shapes

1. **The one-line read.** An original post stating the one claim in
   their piece that is true and underpriced, with the piece linked in
   the first reply. Use this for long-form (Variant, Haun, Pantera,
   UFO Substack, Predicted). The line has to be something only that
   piece says.
2. **The data reply.** A reply inside their own thread, within the
   first thirty minutes of it posting, with one number or one source
   that extends their point. Use this for people with large accounts
   where a quote would be buried.
3. **The lagged original.** Twelve to twenty-four hours after their
   piece, an original on the same theme that cites them by name in the
   body. Use this when the piece did not escape and a quote would not
   ride.

Never: a quote tweet of a mega-viral post more than twenty minutes
old; a generic compliment; a post that works without the piece.
