# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Analytical users evaluating private-company valuation expectations through paper-money markets. They need to understand implied distributions, compare strikes, place precise YES/NO trades, and monitor portfolio exposure without learning a professional terminal first.

## Product Purpose

OpenMarket tests whether a private company’s next reported valuation can behave like a continuously traded price. Success means the implied median, probability ladder, history, execution state, and portfolio remain mathematically coherent and immediately legible before and after every trade.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, analytical, restrained. The product should feel calm under scrutiny: precise enough for market work, approachable enough for a first-time user, and confident without spectacle.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a crypto casino. Avoid neon gradients, token imagery, gamified winnings, confetti, hype language, glowing controls, noisy tickers, or color used as decoration. Do not turn every datum into a card or imitate a cramped legacy terminal.

## Design Principles

1. Put probability and price evidence before decoration.
2. Make every trade consequence inspectable before commitment.
3. Preserve one coherent state across market, chart, ticket, cash, and portfolio.
4. Use familiar controls and dense information without expert-only friction.
5. Treat error, loading, empty, moved-price, and settled states as first-class product states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. All trading controls must be keyboard reachable; focus must remain visible and predictable. Positive and negative movement must never rely on color alone. Charts require textual summaries. Motion must be brief, state-driven, and disabled under reduced-motion preferences. Mobile bottom sheets must trap and restore focus and close with Escape.
