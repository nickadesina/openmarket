## Direction contract
THESIS: The market's whole state reads as one ruled rack of labeled modules — refusing both the glowing dark terminal and the soft-shadow SaaS card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground; near-black compact grotesque; hairline gray rules boxing every module edge to edge; small solid utility-red header tabs; red reserved for prices, movement, selection, and primary actions; tabular numerals; stamped OPEN badges. Recognizable with all content removed.
STORY: An analytical visitor reads implied valuations at a glance, expands a row into its YES/NO ladder, trusts the arithmetic, and clicks through to trade.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar; hairline stat strip of real facts; MARKET BOARD dominant (~2/3) with expandable rows opening inline ladders; LIVE ACTIVITY tape right (~1/3); disclosure footer. Primary action: market rows.
FORM: Japanese contemporary high-density site (challenger, user-picked over assigned pit ticket); seed 3c97be4f. User steer, verbatim: "more minimalistic I think, the site is too full" — density comes from real content only, never invented rows.
